Comprehensive Treatments

Specialized Dental Care

Provisional service offerings based on clinic reference materials. Every procedure is tailored to your individual oral health needs.

Oral Check-up

Thorough visual examination of teeth, gums, and oral mucosa to detect early signs of decay, tartar build-up, and bite irregularities.

  • Comprehensive soft tissue & gum check
  • Cavity and fracture detection
  • Personalized preventive oral health advice

Scaling (Deep Cleaning)

Gentle ultrasonic removal of hardened tartar (calculus) and plaque from around tooth surfaces and beneath the gumline to prevent gum disease.

  • Ultrasonic water-cooled scaling tips
  • Reduces gum swelling and bleeding
  • Protects periodontal ligament health

Tooth Polishing

Prophylactic micro-buffing following scaling to lift external stains caused by tea, coffee, and tobacco, leaving enamel glossy and clean.

  • Gentle stain removal formulation
  • Smooths enamel to resist plaque adhesion
  • Leaves mouth feeling fresh and revitalized

Root Canal Treatment

Specialized therapy to eliminate deep infection inside the tooth pulp, relieve severe toothache, and preserve your natural tooth from extraction.

  • Sterile bio-inert canal obturation
  • Effective pain relief and infection elimination
  • Preserves original natural jaw structure

Aesthetic Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored composite resins bonded seamlessly into cavity preparations to restore natural appearance, bite strength, and chew function.

  • Shade-matched natural tooth aesthetics
  • Mercury-free composite bonding
  • Conservative, tooth-preserving cavity prep

Crown / Cap Restorations

Custom-fabricated prosthetic caps that encase heavily decayed, cracked, or root-canal-treated teeth, protecting against future fractures.

  • High-strength durable restorations
  • Custom bite calibration and alignment
  • Restores full biting and chewing power
